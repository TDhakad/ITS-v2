# Onboarding Docs

Use this article when a new hire or transfer needs help completing standard IT
onboarding.

Onboarding tasks vary by department, but every user should complete identity,
device, collaboration, and security setup before requesting role-specific
systems.

## Day One Checklist

1. Activate the company account.
2. Enroll MFA in the identity portal.
3. Sign in to company email and calendar.
4. Confirm laptop encryption and endpoint protection are active.
5. Connect to the VPN if required for internal resources.
6. Join the required collaboration channels.
7. Review password, data handling, and acceptable use policies.

## Role-Based Access

Engineering users may need repository, cloud, monitoring, and JIRA access.
Support users may need helpdesk, CRM, telephony, and knowledge-base access.
Finance, HR, and legal users should request access through their department
application owners.

Do not grant role-based access before the manager or application owner approves
the request.

## Common Issues

If a new hire cannot sign in, confirm the account start date and MFA enrollment
status. If a laptop is missing required software, route the ticket to endpoint
support with the device serial number and user department.

## Escalation

Escalate to onboarding support when the account is not active on the employee
start date, required hardware has not arrived, or multiple required applications
are missing after manager approval.
